# Relevant extract: Farley, Proposition 1

Source: J. D. Farley, “Quasi-completeness and localizations of polynomial
domains: A conjecture from ‘Open Problems in Commutative Ring Theory’,”
Bulletin of the Korean Mathematical Society 53 (2016), 1613–1615,
DOI `10.4134/BKMS.b140895`, Proposition 1.

Precise statement (faithful paraphrase).  A Noetherian local integral domain
`R` is weakly quasi-complete if and only if every nonzero prime `P` of its
completion `Rhat` has nonzero contraction `P cap R`.

The paper attributes the criterion to Anderson, Corollary 2, part (1), and uses
it to prove that localizations of polynomial rings in at least two variables
are not weakly quasi-complete by constructing a nonzero completion prime with
zero contraction.  Thus the meaning of “prime in the completion contracts
nontrivially” agrees exactly with the generic-formal-fiber terminology used by
Jensen.
